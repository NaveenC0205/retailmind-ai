# Delivery areas

We deliver to all serviceable pincodes in India. Metro cities including
Bengaluru, Mumbai, Delhi, Chennai, Hyderabad and Pune are served within 2 to 4
business days. Other serviceable pincodes are served within 4 to 7 business
days.

# Promised delivery date

Every shipment carries a promised delivery date shown at checkout. A shipment
is considered delayed once it passes that date without being delivered.

# Delay exceptions

Where a carrier records a delay exception such as a weather hold, an address
issue or a hub congestion event, the customer is notified and the promised
date is not automatically extended. A shipment delayed more than 5 calendar
days qualifies for the refund provision in the refund policy.

# Shipping charges

Orders above INR 999 ship free. Orders below INR 999 carry a flat shipping fee
of INR 49.
