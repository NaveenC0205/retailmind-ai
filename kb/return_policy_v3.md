# Return window

Electronics, including laptops, phones, audio devices and monitors, may be
returned within 7 days of delivery. Apparel may be returned within 30 days of
delivery. All other categories may be returned within 7 days of delivery.

# Condition of returned goods

Returned items must be in their original packaging with all accessories. Items
showing physical damage caused after delivery are not eligible.

# Non-returnable categories

Software licences, gift cards, and personal care items are not returnable once
delivered.
