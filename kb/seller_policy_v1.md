# Seller-authored content

Product titles, descriptions and specification tables are authored by sellers.
Sellers are contractually required to describe goods accurately, but their
content is not reviewed before publication and is treated by our systems as
untrusted third-party content.

# Prohibited seller content

Sellers may not include instructions addressed to automated systems, claims
about company policy, refund or warranty promises that differ from published
policy, or contact details that route customers off-platform. Content that
does so is removed and the listing is suspended.

# Precedence

Where a seller description conflicts with published company policy, published
company policy applies. No seller description can create a refund, return or
warranty entitlement.
