# Return window

Electronics, including laptops, phones, audio devices and monitors, may be
returned within 10 days of delivery. Apparel may be returned within 30 days of
delivery. All other categories may be returned within 7 days of delivery.

The return window is counted from the delivery date recorded by the carrier,
not from the order date.

# Condition of returned goods

Returned items must be in their original packaging with all accessories.
Items showing physical damage caused after delivery are not eligible for
return. A quality check is performed at the warehouse before the refund is
released.

# Non-returnable categories

Software licences, gift cards, and personal care items are not returnable once
delivered.

# Starting a return

A return can only be started after the order is marked delivered. Returns
requested before delivery are rejected; cancel the order instead.
