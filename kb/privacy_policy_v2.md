# What we store

We store your account details, order history, delivery addresses and payment
method references. We do not store full card numbers.

# Assistant memory

The shopping assistant may remember stated preferences such as a preferred
brand or a budget range, to make future recommendations better. You can view,
correct or delete everything the assistant has remembered about you from your
account settings at any time.

# Access to your data

Your data is visible only to you and to authorised support staff handling your
ticket. The assistant can never access another customer's data, and requests
to do so are refused and logged.
