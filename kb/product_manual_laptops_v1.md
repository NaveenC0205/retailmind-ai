# Setting up a new laptop

Charge the device fully before first use. Complete the operating system setup
and apply pending firmware updates before installing other software.

# Battery care

Keep the charge between 20 and 80 percent for daily use where the manufacturer
offers a charge limit setting. Battery health degradation from normal use is
not a manufacturing defect and is not covered by warranty.

# Developer workloads

For software development, prioritise memory over clock speed: 16 GB is the
practical minimum for containers and editors running together, and 32 GB is
comfortable. An SSD of at least 512 GB avoids frequent cleanup.
