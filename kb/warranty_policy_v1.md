# Manufacturer warranty

Laptops and monitors carry a 12-month manufacturer warranty from the date of
delivery. Phones carry a 12-month warranty. Audio devices carry a 6-month
warranty.

# What warranty covers

Warranty covers manufacturing defects and component failure under normal use.
It does not cover physical damage, liquid damage, or damage from unauthorised
repair.

# Making a warranty claim

Warranty claims are handled by the manufacturer's service network. Our support
team can raise a ticket and share the nearest authorised service centre.
Warranty is separate from the return window: an item outside its return window
may still be under warranty.
