# Coupon usage

One coupon may be applied per order. Coupons cannot be combined with each
other. A coupon applies to the order subtotal before shipping charges.

# Minimum order value

Most coupons carry a minimum order value shown alongside the code. If the
subtotal falls below the minimum after a return, the discount is recovered
from the refunded amount.

# Expiry

Coupons expire at 23:59 IST on their stated end date. Expired coupons cannot
be reinstated by support.
