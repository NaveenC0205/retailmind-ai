# Refund timelines

Once a returned item passes the warehouse quality check, the refund is
initiated within 2 business days. The amount reaches the original payment
method within 5 to 7 business days for cards and within 3 business days for
UPI.

# Refunds for cancelled orders

An order cancelled before dispatch is refunded in full, automatically, within
3 business days. No return is required.

# Refunds for delayed shipments

If a shipment is more than 5 calendar days late against its promised delivery
date, the customer may request a full refund without returning the item, or
choose to wait. This applies only where the carrier has recorded a delay
exception.

# Approval thresholds

Refunds above INR 50,000 require approval by a human support manager before
they are released. The customer is told a reference number while approval is
pending.
